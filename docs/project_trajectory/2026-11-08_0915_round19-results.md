### rwm0.2 seed 1: why it stopped after learning

Seed 1 learned at update 790. In the last 100 updates before it stopped (2,901-3,000) it held probe success 0.97-1.00 with 476-489 active units.
- From update 2,334 onward, single updates had a non-finite KL divergence (some minibatch loss not finite).
- At update 3,001 the rollout was not finite six times in a row, and the guard stopped the run.
- Seed 3 followed the same course: first non-finite update at 2,595, stopped at 3,400. Its last saved weights
  are entirely non-finite.
- Seed 2 ran all 2,000 stage-2 updates with no non-finite event.

**What the records exclude.**
- *A warm-up artefact at the stage boundary.* The restart at update 2,001 repeats the 50-update learning-rate
  ramp, which ends at 2,050. The first non-finite events came 284 (seed 1) and 545 (seed 3) updates after the
  ramp ended. In stage 1 all three seeds ran 2,000 updates with no non-finite event.
- *Recurrent weights grown under the penalty.*
  - The penalty term stays flat: 0.0025-0.0028 in seed 1 throughout stage 2.
  - The recurrent weights stay bounded and smaller than the controls'. Seed 1 at update 2,000 has spectral
    radius 3.02, largest |W| 0.068 (the cap is 0.047) and 1.8% of entries above the cap. At update 3,000 those
    are 3.22, 0.069 and 1.8%.
  - The controls at 2,000 have spectral radius 4.2-4.4, largest |W| 0.29-0.31 and 21-23% of entries above
    0.047.

**What the records cannot separate.** The other part of the restart is that Adam's moments start afresh at
update 2,001. Their estimates rebuild over the following few hundred updates, which is the window in which
both seeds failed. Late-training instability under the penalty, with no restart, would look the same.

Non-finite events are more common in stage 2 across the whole package. 24 of the 63 continued networks have a
guard restore in stage 2, against 12 of the same 63 in stage 1. Restores also occur in stage 2 of the controls
(ReLU seeds 1, 4, 5) and of softplus seeds 2 and 3, none of which stopped.

**The test that separates the two:** rerun rwm0.2 seed 1's stage 2 with `INIT_ADAM=true`, which restores
stage 1's Adam state; the AdamState file exists on Spock. If the run is not finite again near update 2,300,
the fresh optimiser state is not the cause. About 5.8 GPU-h. Not submitted; it is for the coordinator.
