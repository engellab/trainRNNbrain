# Round 18 text package: figures for the paper (dead-relu-paper #201, PR on wp/r18-text)

The figure half of the round-18 text package. Numbers come from the results entry
`2026-11-07_0235_round18-results.md` (rules `2026-11-07_0100_round18-rules.md`) and the large-chain ablation
entry `2026-11-07_0212_maze-ablations-large-chains.md`; nothing new was computed except the bootstrap below.

**Fig. 2a and Extended Data Maze c (49a0805, 6e4d03a).** The Maze power law is fitted at N >= 500, the range of
3BFF and CDDM (coordinator's decision 2 on #201). Printout of `fig_paper_F2_size.py`: learned point
b = 0.345, bootstrap 95% [0.314, 0.380]; end of training b = 0.410 [0.378, 0.445]. Over every size from 250 (second
reading): 0.473 [0.436, 0.512] and 0.516 [0.486, 0.548]. G10 printed the end-of-training interval as 0.378-0.444:
same fit, same seed, a different draw order in the bootstrap, so the upper end differs in the third decimal
(0.444 against 0.445). The paper uses the figure script's printout, 0.38 to 0.45.

**Fig. 5c (49a0805).** Two sub-columns: 3BFF as before, CDDM prune-and-duplicate (per network) and read-out
dropout (line) at the floor crossing from `data/round18/g6_reads.json`. Fold changes per size equal G6
(prune-and-duplicate 1.37, 1.52, 1.94, 2.11; read-out dropout 1.29, 1.26, 1.26, 1.02). The bottom row draws the
median R^2 change per size (-0.0023 to +0.0020 for prune-and-duplicate); G6 reports means (-0.004 to +0.002).

**Fig. 5d (49a0805).** One removal on all three tasks: the k added units removed with the inactive units in
place, against k random active units (median of 5). 3BFF: 19 networks of `a3_N1000.json` (unchanged). CDDM:
14 networks of G3. Maze: 12 networks of G4. The 570-unit Maze form is no longer drawn. Random removal lower than
the added-unit removal, per network: CDDM 14 of 14, Maze 12 of 12, 3BFF 17 of 19 (the exceptions are one
read-out dropout network, 0.228 against 0.193, and one prune-and-duplicate network whose R^2 after removing its
added units is -4,902; per technique, the 3BFF median is lower for five of the six techniques, as before). Two CDDM rate-penalty networks lie beyond the left edge at nearly the same
height; they are drawn as one edge marker with the number 2.

**Extended Data Maze d.** The script draws every learned chain end in `data/rl_maze_ablation`, so the
N = 2,000 and 4,000 ablations of b6ae40f now appear (8 networks in all).
