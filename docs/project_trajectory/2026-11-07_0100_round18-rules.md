`2026-11-07_0047_experiments-across-tasks-audit.md` (dead-relu-paper#201) lists the missing cells. This
entry fixes, for every priority-1 analysis of that audit that runs on networks that already exist, the
quantity, the read-out point, the networks, the controls, the threshold or direction rule, and which
paper claim each result would change. It is committed and pushed before any of these numbers is
computed; the results entry cites it. No network is trained in this package, and the paper's text is not
edited.

**Scope.** G1 to G10 and G13 to G15. **G16 is skipped**: it needs weights at update 2,000 and the
participation of a continuation run, which is training (G11). The Maze gradient analysis (G20) is
priority 2 and is skipped; the silent-unit counterpart (G14) is priority 1 and is done.

**What I saw before writing.** While locating the data I printed the per-network `active_75k` and
`r2_75k` of the N = 1,000 cells of `data/remedy_reads_31oct/remedies_catalogue.json`, the numbers from
which Supplementary Table 3a's means are computed. The G2 rule below is the paper's own criterion with
the tolerances fixed by the package brief (0.005, 0.01, 0.02), so it has no free choice that those
numbers could have steered. No other outcome below was looked at.

Terms. *Criterion*: the paper's technique criterion (M09, quoted under G2). *k added units*: a technique
network's number of active units minus the control networks' mean, taken as its k least participating
active units (the audit's definition, Results 5). *Learned* (Maze): last probe success at or above 0.9.
*Learned point*: the first probe from which probe success stays at or above 0.9 for five probes.

### G1. 3BFF rate penalty at λ_frm = 0.01 and 0.03 (and 0.1 re-read the same way)

- **Networks.** `NBitFlipFlop_remedy_grid/EqType=h_N=1000_arm=frm{0.01,0.03,0.1}`, 3 each, N = 1,000,
  trained 200k in one piece, snapshots every 1,000 iterations in `c1_snapshots/NBitFlipFlop_remedy_grid`.
- **Read-out (reused, M09).** 75,000 iterations. Active units: the participation probe nearest 75k in the
  run's trace (as `read_remedies.read_one`, the catalogue's `active_75k`). Held-out R²: `heldout_r2` on the
  `it0075000` snapshot, 36 noise draws, σ_w = 0, the seeded held-out batch (the catalogue's `r2_75k`).
  λ = 0.1 is re-read with 36 draws so that all three values are measured like the other techniques; its
  9-draw value from the removal analysis is printed beside it.
- **Controls (reused, M09).** "The four default 3BFF networks of Fig. 1 (236 to 288 active units; R² in
  the three with weights saved at 75,000 iterations)", the catalogue's N = 1,000 `control` cell.
- **Config check.** Each arm's saved config is compared with the control's on `read_remedies.SHARED_FIELDS`;
  a difference is reported.
- **Rule.** The criterion, applied at each value: pass if every network's active count exceeds 288 (the
  largest control count) and the mean R² is at least the control mean minus 0.01. The 0.03 and 0.1
  values are checked against `read_b3.json` (0.03) and `a3_N1000.json` (0.1); a disagreement above 0.002
  in R² or 5 units is investigated before any result is reported.
- **Claims.** If λ = 0.01 or 0.03 passes, the rate penalty meets the criterion on 3BFF (one-value rule,
  below), and with its CDDM and Maze passes "Prune-and-duplicate is the one technique that meets the
  criterion on all three tasks" (abstract, Results 5, Discussion, M09) is false at tolerance 0.01. If
  both fail, the headline stands and Supplementary Table 3a and the verdict table gain the two values.

### G2. The per-network criterion on every 3BFF value of Supplementary Table 3a

- **Criterion (reused, quoted from M09).** "A technique meets the criterion on a task when every network
  trained with it has more active units than every control network, and the mean task performance of
  these networks is within a tolerance of the control networks' mean: 0.01 in held-out R² (3BFF,
  CDDM)" ... "A technique run at several values on a task meets the criterion on that task if it meets it
  at one of them." The R² test is one-sided, as in `fig_paper_F5_tasks.rule`.
- **Networks.** Every N = 1,000 row of Supplementary Table 3a (`table_rule_settings.collect()`): every
  catalogue cell of the five techniques (rate penalty, rate penalty with sparsity, noisy synapses,
  rescaling, prune-and-duplicate, read-out dropout) with role `topup` or `b2`, plus the rate-penalty values
  of G1. Re-initialisation (`redraw`) is a setting, not a technique, and is left out. Every catalogue cell
  has the standard network (no field of `SHARED_FIELDS` differs from the control; checked in the
  catalogue). Rows at N = 500, 2,000 and 4,000 are outside the criterion, which is defined at N = 1,000;
  they are not judged.
- **Read-out and controls.** As G1: catalogue `active_75k` and `r2_75k` (36 draws); the four default
  networks.
- **Details fixed now.** The mean R² is over every network with a 75k score, including networks with
  R² ≤ 0 (the criterion says "mean task performance of these networks"; the table's mean leaves them
  out, and a cell where the two differ is flagged). A cell with fewer 75k reads than runs is judged on
  the networks read and flagged.
- **Rule.** Each value: pass or fail at tolerances 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02. Each technique on 3BFF: pass if
  any value passes. Then, with the CDDM and Maze verdicts of `fig_paper_F5_tasks.tolerance_sensitivity`
  (unchanged; Maze at its tolerance 0.05), the list of techniques passing on all three tasks at each
  R² tolerance.
- **Claims.** "Prune-and-duplicate is the one technique that meets the criterion on all three tasks"
  (and "the only technique ... at all three tolerances", M09) is false at a tolerance if another
  technique's list entry includes all three tasks there. "Read-out dropout and the rate penalty with the
  sparsity penalty meet the criterion in CDDM networks only, and noisy synapses and rescaling in none"
  (Results 5) changes for each technique that passes on 3BFF at 0.01 at some value.

### G3. Removing the added units in CDDM networks (the 3BFF A3 procedure)

- **Procedure (reused, `a3_remedy_ablation.ablate_net`).** Participation on a noise-free training batch,
  active = p ≥ 0.05·q95(p) on those weights; a unit removed by zeroing its outgoing recurrent and output
  weights; held-out R² under the training noise, σ_w = 0, 9 seeded draws. Scores: intact; every inactive
  unit removed; the k added units removed with the inactive units left in place; k active units removed
  at random (5 sets, median); every unit but the n_ctrl most participating removed (`r2_keep_ctrl`, the
  Maze form); and N − n_ctrl units removed at random from all N (5 sets, median; new key
  `r2_random_keep`, drawn from a separate generator so the existing keys are unchanged).
- **Networks and controls.** CDDM, N = 1,000. Prune-and-duplicate and read-out dropout:
  `CDDM_paper_grid/EqType=h_N=1000_arm={duplication,mute}` at their final weights (100k; no 75k weights
  exist), k against the offline count of `CDDM_paper_grid/..._arm=control` at its 100k weights. Rate
  penalty: λ = 0.1 (`CDDM_std_g0_penalties_bias0/..._pen=frm0.1_iters=75000`, 3) and λ = 0.2
  (`CDDM_std_g0_bias0_c75000/EqType=h_N=1000_LmbdRWS=0_LmbdFR=0.2`, 5), both at 75k, k against the
  offline count of the Figure 3a control `CDDM_std_g0_bias0_c75000/..._LmbdRWS=0_LmbdFR=0` (5) at 75k.
  Arm and control are always read at the same iteration. (New choice: the criterion pairs λ = 0.1 with
  the paper-grid control at 75k from traces, but that control has no 75k weights.)
- **Rule (new thresholds).** Per network, drop = intact R² − R² with the k added units removed. A
  technique's output depends on the added units if drop ≥ 0.10 in every network; it does not depend on
  them if drop < 0.01 (the criterion's tolerance) in every network; otherwise mixed. Reported beside it:
  whether k random active units lower R² further (median over networks), as Results 5 states for 3BFF.
- **Claims.** Results 5 says "We did not make these removals in CDDM networks." If "depends", "in 3BFF and
  Maze networks" becomes "in 3BFF, CDDM and Maze networks". If "does not depend", CDDM is stated as the
  exception, and the sentence keeps its two tasks.

### G4. Removing the added units in Maze networks, 3BFF form and 570-unit form

- **Networks.** `Maze_interventions/frm{0.03,0.1,0.3}/s{1,2,3}`: every rate-penalty network that finished
  2,000 updates (status in `read_maze_arms.json`; the learned flag is reported, every finished network is
  counted, as the criterion counts them), and `Maze_interventions/duplicate/s{1,2,3}`. Final weights
  (update 2,000; no other weights exist). Noisy-synapse networks are not in the brief and are not removed.
- **Procedure (reused, `rl_maze_ablate.py`).** Active set from the clean probe (128 drops, the training's
  probe seed); a unit removed by zeroing its column of W_rec and of W_out; success under the training noise
  with the mean action, 256 fixed drops × 4 seeded draws. k = n_active − 430, with 430 the mean of the three
  control networks (`reference_techniques` of `read_maze_arms.json`, the criterion's controls). Scores:
  intact; every inactive unit removed; the k added units removed with the inactive units left in place;
  k active units at random (5 sets, median); the N − 430 = 570 least participating units removed; 570 units
  at random from all N (5 sets, median). New code: two orders added to `rl_maze_ablate.py`.
- **Check.** For the five networks already removed, the 570-unit values are compared with the values
  interpolated in `data/rl_maze_ablation_interventions`; a difference above 0.03 is investigated.
- **Rule (new thresholds).** Per network, drop = intact success − success with the k added units removed.
  A technique's output depends on the added units if drop ≥ 0.10 (twice the Maze tolerance 0.05) in every
  network; does not depend if drop < 0.05 in every network; otherwise mixed. Reported beside it: whether k
  random active units lower success further.
- **Claims.** Results 5: "The output of a network trained with a technique depends on the units the
  technique adds, in 3BFF and Maze networks" rests on five rate-penalty networks and a different removal.
  The result gives the Maze value under the 3BFF removal for every finished rate-penalty network and for
  prune-and-duplicate; if prune-and-duplicate does not depend, the sentence is scoped to the rate penalty
  on Maze.

### G5. The Maze form of the removal in 3BFF networks

- **Networks.** Every N = 1,000 network of `a3_N1000.json` (the 3BFF A3 set, 75k), re-run with
  `a3_remedy_ablation.py` extended by `r2_random_keep` (G3). The re-run must reproduce the stored
  `r2_full`, `r2_recruited` and `r2_keep_ctrl` within 0.002 (same seeds); otherwise investigated.
- **Rule (new, descriptive).** Per technique, the median over networks of `r2_keep_ctrl` (inactive units
  plus the k added units removed) and of `r2_random_keep` (as many units removed at random from all N).
  The Maze statement "removing 570 randomly chosen units ... lowers it further" holds for a 3BFF technique
  if `r2_random_keep` < `r2_keep_ctrl` in every network of that technique.
- **Claims.** Fig. 5d and Results 5 can then state one removal on both tasks. No claim changes direction
  unless `r2_keep_ctrl` is within 0.01 of intact for some technique.

### G7. Flat-loss test on CDDM

- **Test (reused unchanged, `c3_flat_loss_units.py`, entry 2026-10-15_0955, restated 2026-10-31_1900).**
  Window 150k to the end of training; loss change on 5k-block medians, flat if |change| < 3% per 100k;
  units lost per 100k = minus the least-squares slope of the active count on every probe at ≥ 150k,
  "decreasing" if ≥ 5. Per size: decreasing if at least two thirds of the flat networks decrease; not
  decreasing if at least two thirds do not; otherwise mixed.
- **Networks.** `CDDM_std_g0_drift_bias0/EqType=h_N={500,1000}_iters=200000` and `..._N=2000_iters=300000`,
  3 each (standard network, one piece, on the Mac). N = 100 (50k) does not reach 150k and is left out.
- **Claims.** Results 2: "In most 3BFF networks, the number of active units decreases even while the
  training loss is flat." If CDDM is "decreasing" over the networks pooled, the sentence covers 3BFF and
  CDDM; if "not decreasing", the paper states that CDDM differs.

### G8. Maze: flat training return after the learned point

- **Networks.** The learned default networks of the Maze size series (ReLU, `Maze_suite`, N = 250 to
  4,000; the N = 2,000 and 4,000 chains joined by `common.maze_load_runs`).
- **Quantity (new).** Training discounted return per update (`Progress.json`). B0 = median over updates
  1-100; BL = median over the 100 updates ending at the learned update; rise R = BL − B0. Window: from the
  learned update to the end. D = median of the last 100 updates − median of the first 100 updates of the
  window, per 1,000 updates (divided by the distance between the two block centres in thousands of
  updates). Flat if |D| < 0.03·R per 1,000 updates (3% of the rise; the return has no natural zero, so the
  3BFF "3% of the loss" becomes 3% of the rise). Active units lost per 1,000 updates = minus the
  least-squares slope of the clean-probe active count on every probe in the window; "decreasing" if ≥ 5
  (the 3BFF bar, units per half of the standard budget). Windows shorter than 300 updates are not tested.
  A sensitivity line drops the 100 updates after each chain junction (Adam restarts with a warm-up there).
- **Rule.** Over the flat networks, "decreasing while the return is flat" if at least two thirds decrease;
  "not decreasing" if at least two thirds do not; otherwise mixed.
- **Claims.** The Results 2 flat-loss sentence: a "decreasing" verdict gives Maze as a second task; "not
  decreasing" is stated as a difference. With 2,000 updates at N ≤ 1,000 the windows are 700-1,300
  updates; the result is labelled as short-window until G11 exists.

### G14. Silent and low-active units on Maze

- **Definition (reused, Methods "Participation; active, inactive and silent units").** "an inactive unit
  silent if its net input x_i is negative on every time step after the initial state, on every trial of a
  noise-free batch." Low-active: inactive and not silent.
- **Two noise-free batches.** (a) the clean probe (128 drops, the training's probe seed, mean action, every
  RNN step before the cheese); (b) the frozen rollout of the audit: 256 episodes, seed 20261107, recurrent,
  input and output noise off, actions sampled from N(mean, 0.5²) as in training, every RNN step while the
  episode runs. Silence on (b) is the one that sets the policy-gradient of that rollout to zero for the
  unit's incoming and outgoing weights; inactive units are those of the clean probe.
- **Networks.** The three N = 1,000 default Maze networks at update 2,000.
- **Rule (new).** Maze is grouped with CDDM if every network has at least one silent unit on (b) and the
  silent units are at least 10% of its inactive units; with 3BFF if no network has a silent unit on (b);
  otherwise intermediate, with the counts.
- **Claims.** C4 and Results 4 ("on CDDM 253-310 of 1,000 units are silent; on 3BFF none is") gain the Maze
  count. No gradient is computed (G20 is priority 2).

### G15. Maze: inactive units active again later, from the logs

- **Networks.** The learned default networks of the Maze size series (as G8).
- **Quantity (counterpart of Fig. 4c).** Reference: the first stored participation vector (every 50
  updates) at or after the learned update. Later: the stored vectors 50 and 700 updates after the reference
  (about 400 and 5,600 optimiser steps; 3BFF and CDDM use 500 and 5,500 iterations). Active in each vector
  by p ≥ 0.05·q95(p) of that vector. Reported: of the units inactive at the reference, the number and
  fraction active later. A network whose reference + 700 lies beyond its last vector is reported and not
  counted at 700.
- **Rule (reuses the 3BFF bound "fewer than a tenth").** "Inactive units do not become active again" holds
  on Maze if the fraction at +700 is below 0.10 in every network counted; it is contradicted if the fraction
  is at least 0.10 in more than half; otherwise mixed. The split into silent and low-active units needs
  weights at the reference (G16) and is not made.
- **Claims.** C7 and Results 4 ("inactive units do not become active again"), now from 3BFF and CDDM, gain or
  lose Maze.
