**What was built** (`trainRNNbrain/trainer/TrainerPPO.py`, `configs/trainer/trainer_ppo.yaml`,
keys named as in the supervised trainer): the rate penalty (`lambda_frm`, `frm_args`: the soft-max

sparsity penalty (`lambda_rws`); noisy synapses (`model.sigma_w`: W_rec and W_inp multiplied by

kind mute: one mask per rollout from the RNN's own `get_dropout_mask` on the participation EMA,

independent agent on issue #5 (approve; four follow-ups applied: synaptic-noise seeds below 2^32,
