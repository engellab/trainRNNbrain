## Why B and D disagree (diagnostic, not set in advance)

Every stage restarts Adam with fresh moments and a 50-update warm-up (`INIT_ADAM=false`, as in every earlier stage).
Over updates 4,000-20,000 (eight stages), the number of active units falls within stages by 66 to 234 units in total
per network and rises at stage boundaries by 3 to 73 units in total: the restart reactivates some units, which then
fall again during the stage. D's within-stage slopes therefore include this recovery and overstate the decrease of an
uninterrupted run; A and B span stage boundaries. The paper uses B as the rule says and reports D beside it with this
reason. The same restarts were in the 4,000-12,000 records; there they work against the decrease, so that reading
(6 of 6 decreasing) is not inflated by them.
