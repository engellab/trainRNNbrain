*Floor crossing*: the first iteration after which the smoothed noisy training loss stays at or below 1.05 (or
1.10, 1.20) times its fitted floor through 1.2 times that iteration. *Active unit*: participation
p = std(r) + q0.9(|r|) on a noise-free pass, active if p ≥ 0.05·q95(p). *Learned point* (Maze): the first probe
from which success stays at or above 0.9 for five probes in a row, in a record whose last probe is also at or
above 0.9.

### Against the outcomes the rules entry called surprising

1. Mean at N = 250 below the N = 100 mean or above the N = 500 mean: **no** on either task (3BFF 82 < 134 < 205;
   CDDM 90 < 183 < 298).
2. A CDDM network at N = 250 with 125 or fewer active units: **no** (174-188). On 3BFF, for which the rules entry
   set no label, one network has exactly 125.
3. An N = 250 network without a floor crossing: **no**.
4. Held-out R² more than 0.01 below the N = 100 and N = 500 networks: **no**.
5. A Maze network at N = 100 not learned within 8,000 updates: **no**; all four learned by update 1,780.
6. Maze mean at N = 100 above the N = 250 mean (150): **no** (68).
7. A new every-size exponent outside the old interval: **yes**, in five of the nine fits: CDDM at 1.10× and 1.20×,
   and Maze at all three read points.

Outcome 7 needs a reading. The bootstrap interval reflects only the spread between networks within each size;
it does not cover the error of fitting one straight line on log axes to means that do not lie on one. The
CDDM means bend: the slope between N = 100 and 250 is 0.78, between 250 and 500 0.70, and over N ≥ 500 0.44. A
point added inside the fitted range then moves b by more than the resampling spread when that spread is small
(CDDM intervals are 0.01-0.02 wide). For Maze, the N = 100 mean (68) lies below the old every-size law's
extrapolation to N = 100 (117, more than the 100 units available), so adding it steepens the slope. Neither
result bears on the primary fits, which use N ≥ 500 only.

**An expectation stated in the rules entry that failed.** The entry expected "nearly every unit" of the N = 100
Maze networks to be active, by analogy with the supervised tasks at N = 100 (82 and 90 of 100). The measured mean
is 68 of 100, and it is close to the count after update 1 (56-65). On Maze, a smaller share of the units is
active at N = 100 than on 3BFF and CDDM at N = 100, and the untrained network already has that share.
