# Round-22 polish: three figure fixes from the round-21 committee (dead-relu-paper #223)

Branch `wp/r22-polish` from main 94c8ab3. No data changed and no number changed: each script's printout is
identical before and after the change, except the two new printout lines of Fig. 5d named below.

**Fig. 4d,e: young networks are pentagons.** Reviewer S (committee #217, figures item 2) asked for one
meaning per marker shape. The triangle means prune-and-duplicate in Fig. 5 and Extended Data Figs. 6, 9
and 10, and it also meant the young networks of Fig. 4d,e. `fig_paper_F4_noreturn.py`, `SRC_STYLE["young"]`:
marker `"^"` became `"p"` (pentagon), a shape used nowhere else in the paper. Fig. 1d and Extended Data
Fig. 1 keep their triangle for random removal; the young networks were the cheaper change, because Fig. 4
redraws on the Mac and Fig. 1 needs Della.

**Fig. 5d, 3BFF sub-panel: two rate-penalty circles drawn beside their values.** Two rate-penalty networks
(removal of the k least participating active units / random removal: 0.218 / 0.106 and 0.184 / 0.128,
printout panel d) lie 0.5 mm and 0.3 mm from a prune-and-duplicate network (0.233 / 0.104) and a read-out
dropout network (0.176 / 0.127). Their see-through faces printed grey, which Reviewer S read as a third
kind of mark (figures item 1). `fig_paper_F5_tasks.py`: `D_MOVE_PT` and `displace()` draw these two circles
offset by (-6.0, -4.0) and (-4.4, -4.6) pt, at (0.052, 0.175) and (0.088, 0.134), each with a thin line
from the rim of the triangle that covers its value to its own rim. Both stay above the diagonal, on the
side of their values. A 65% white face (Extended Data Fig. 10b's) was tried first and still printed grey.
The caption names the two displaced markers.

**Fig. 2f, right panel: the label of the dashed line.** The label "−5 per 100,000" touched its dashed line
(Reviewer S figures item 3; 3 pixels at 800 dpi between the label's lowest ink and the line, about
0.1 mm). One value changed in `fig_paper_F2_size.py`, `panel_flat`: the label's y position from -4.0 to
-1.0 (data units); the gap is now 23 pixels at 800 dpi, about 0.7 mm. Nothing else in Fig. 2 changed; the
N = 250 and N = 100 integration package redraws Fig. 2 and keeps this value.
