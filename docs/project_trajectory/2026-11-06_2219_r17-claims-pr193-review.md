### The check of the continued decrease, from the raw records

Rule, as in `2026-11-01_1000`: every network with a finite floor crossing and without the rate
penalty, counted once by the seed in its name; active units at the stored probe nearest the
crossing against the last probe. Second read: the median over the last 2% of the probes. Counted
from the raw fields of `data/fig_paper_F2_fails_cache.pkl`, and for the optimizer comparison from
each run's `*_ParticipationTrace.pkl` (active by 5% of the q95 of participation, code written for
this count; every value equals `b5_read_cache.pkl`'s `act_floor` and `act_end`). The crossings
themselves come from the pipeline.

| set | networks | fewer at the last probe | fewer by the 2% median |
|---|---|---|---|
| CDDM setting sweeps | 91 | 91 | 91 |
| 3BFF input-weight norm (15) and re-initialisation (10) | 25 | 25 | 25 |
| 3BFF optimizer comparison | 11 | 10 | 9 |
| all | 127 | 126 | 125 |

The two optimizer networks that differ are both SGD at learning rate 3, weight decay 1e-6, and
neither learned the task:

| seed | crossing | at the crossing | last probe | 2% median | final R^2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2246054572 | 27,376 | 124 | 198 | 244 | 0.345 |
| 824557376 | 21,389 | 219 | 201 | 260 | 0.302 |

So the "10 of 11" in the review depends on reading one last probe. s824557376 falls at that probe
and rises by the median. The paper reports both.

Coverage. CDDM: eight of ten settings (weight decay, input-weight norm, cap, activity noise,
spectral radius, magnitude penalty, metabolic penalty, activation without tanh), plus
re-initialisation. Sparsity penalty alone: 0 of 5 networks have a crossing. CDDM optimizer sweep
and CDDM tanh: not in the cache, and their read-out files hold no floor read.

3BFF optimizer comparison: the cache holds 38 of the 39 finished folders. The 39th, SGD learning
rate 3, weight decay 0, s2560134940, diverged (score `nan` in its folder name; participation
non-finite at 149 of 2,000 probes, the first at iteration 1,600; 29,968 non-finite losses).
`common.read_run` gives it a crossing at 30,441 with 128 active units and an end count of 0, which
is an artefact of the non-finite trace, so it is left out of the check; `b5_read.py` leaves it out
too. Counted as a fall it would make 127 of 128; the paper does not count it.

Other 3BFF setting sweeps (weight decay, activity noise, spectral radius, penalties, activation,
caps): their read-out files hold the 75,000-iteration read only. The earlier reason, "trained for
75,000 iterations", was false as a reason (the caps 0.1, 0.5, 2 ran to 150,000, and CDDM sweeps of
75,000 are in the check). No reason was verified, so the text states only that they were not read
at a floor crossing.

### The Maze control reference

`fig_paper_F5_tasks.third_control_sensitivity` prints the verdicts with all three control networks
in the reference. Control success 0.992, 0.977, 0.727: mean 0.984 over the two learned, 0.898 over
all three (0.8984; the review's 0.899 is a rounding slip; `2026-11-05_0812` already had 0.898).
Verdicts that change: noisy synapses on Maze (-0.062 fails -> +0.023 works; 451-478 active against
449) and the rate penalty at 0.1 (-0.120 fails -> -0.034 works). Only the noisy-synapse change moves
a technique's verdict, since the rate penalty passes at 0.03 and 0.3 already: "two on no task" would
become one.

The learned-control reference was fixed in `2026-11-05_0812`, the entry holding the first read-out,
which printed the all-controls means beside it. It was therefore not set before those networks were
measured, and M09 says so. The criterion itself, and "a run that does not learn counts against the
technique", are older (issue #5).
